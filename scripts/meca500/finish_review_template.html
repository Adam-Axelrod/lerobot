Where does a finish count as on target?

Each tile is the final microscope frame of one trial, cropped to 1.29 mm around the target and enlarged without smoothing, so every block is one camera pixel. Mark each one Success, Borderline or Fail by your own standard. Trials are shuffled and labelled with neutral IDs; the measured distance stays hidden until you reveal it, so it cannot steer the call.

The tiles mix scripted teacher demos, the two finish policies, the learned refinement, and the 11 Nov runs that pressed into the dish. Verdicts save as you click.
